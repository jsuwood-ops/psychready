Solutions to barriers in psychiatric services

Approaches drawn from WHO guidance, collaborative care models, and telepsychiatry literature. Adapt them to your local context and regulations.

Workforce shortages

  • Hub-and-spoke telepsychiatry for ED and inpatient consults.
  • Expand the team with psychiatric mental health nurse practitioners and clearly defined roles.
  • Regional training pipelines and rural residency or fellowship tracks.
  • Retention: flexible schedules, wellbeing support, shared caseloads.

Rural & underserved access

  • Telepsychiatry with local nursing or tech support and in-person backup.
  • Task-shifting screening and follow-up to trained primary-care and community staff (mhGAP approach).
  • Partnerships with community mental health, peer support, and mobile crisis teams.

Integration with primary care, ED & medical floors

  • Collaborative care: screening, care manager, scheduled psychiatric case review.
  • Shared protocols and interdisciplinary huddles.
  • Consultation-liaison services, on-site or virtual.

Pathways & continuity

  • Standardized ED and inpatient triage, disposition, and medication-start protocols.
  • Formal referral agreements with closed-loop handoffs.
  • Care navigators and follow-up after discharge.

Funding & reimbursement

  • Use billable collaborative-care and telehealth codes where available.
  • Track boarding hours, readmissions, and time-to-consult to build the business case.
  • Grants, state behavioral health funds, and quality incentive programs.

Stigma & culture

  • Facility-wide training on recognizing and responding to mental illness.
  • Patient and family involvement in service design.
  • Leadership champions who keep behavioral health a visible priority.

Technology & infrastructure

  • Reliable video, EHR integration, and interpreter access.
  • Simple workflows that busy staff will actually use.
  • Continuous monitoring of access and outcome metrics.
