About PsychReady

Mission

Help facilities build confident, competent, and humane behavioral health teams through practical, evidence-informed training.

What our content is based on

Training modules are aligned first with three U.S. organizations:

  • American Psychiatric Association (APA) — APA Clinical Practice Guidelines, APA Telepsychiatry resources
  • Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) — SAMHSA National Behavioral Health Crisis Care Guidance, SAMHSA Trauma-Informed Approaches, SAMHSA 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, SAMHSA Substance Use Treatment Options
  • National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH) — NIMH Ask Suicide-Screening Questions (ASQ) Toolkit, NIMH Suicide Prevention, NIMH Mental Health Medications
  • WHO mhGAP Intervention Guide and training manuals
  • Inpatient mental health quality standards (for example, Royal College of Psychiatrists' quality networks)
  • NICE guidance on violence and aggression
  • Published research on simulation and Behavioral Skills Training in psychiatric education
  • Collaborative care and telepsychiatry literature
  • WHO QualityRights human-rights guidance

We avoid made-up statistics. When we cite results, we name the setting and its limits. PsychReady is independent and not endorsed by APA, SAMHSA, or NIMH.

Standards & sources

  • American Psychiatric Association (APA): APA Clinical Practice Guidelines, APA Telepsychiatry resources
