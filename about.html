About PsychReady

Mission

Help facilities build confident, competent, and humane behavioral health teams through practical, evidence-informed training.

What our content is based on

  • American Psychiatric Association (APA) clinical practice guidelines
  • SAMHSA crisis care, trauma-informed, 988, and substance use guidance
  • NIMH suicide prevention (including the ASQ toolkit) and mental health medication resources
  • WHO mhGAP Intervention Guide and training manuals
  • Inpatient mental health quality standards (for example, Royal College of Psychiatrists' quality networks)
  • NICE guidance on violence and aggression
  • Published research on simulation and Behavioral Skills Training in psychiatric education
  • Collaborative care and telepsychiatry literature
  • WHO QualityRights human-rights guidance

We avoid made-up statistics. When we cite results, we name the setting and its limits.

Disclaimer

Educational use only. PsychReady content is for staff education and program planning. It is not clinical advice and is not a substitute for accredited training, local policy, or professional judgment.
