How the training works

  1. Learn — short focused content per competency.
  2. Practice — Behavioral Skills Training: instruct, model, rehearse, feedback.
  3. Simulate — interprofessional scenarios with structured debrief.
  4. Measure — observed competence and operational outcomes, not just attendance.
